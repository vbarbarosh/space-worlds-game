# World Explorer: chapters

The story chapter by chapter. Acts I and II are the 16 chapters the game has
today: their stages and rewards are the game's own, and the story text around
them (the thread of the Charter) is new. The career lines at the end are new.
The frame is in [README.md](README.md).

## Act I. The Broken Frontier

You learn a trade in each world and open the route from Haven to Eclipse,
where the Charter was lost.

### 1. A pilot earns their wings · Haven · ◆ 306
Scan → mine → courier: *Calibrate the mining scanner → Extract trial ore → Return the analysis to Haven*

Haven Anchorage hands out licences only to pilots who can bring ore it can
trust. The dock master tells you about the old guild that once flew these
lanes, and about the gate to Verdant that nobody uses any more.

### 2. Thorns in the canopy · Verdant · ◆ 522
Scan → hunt → recover → courier: *Locate the swarm nest → Break the swarm patrol → Retrieve the nest telemetry → Bring telemetry back to Haven*

The Thorn Swarm has grown around the Canopy Exchange. Its nest telemetry
shows a signal that repeats on an old guild frequency: someone is still
broadcasting from far beyond Verdant.

### 3. The refinery run · Dustfall · ◆ 540
Courier → defend → courier: *Deliver the sealed navigation core → Hold the refinery relay → Return the refinery response*

The guild's navigation cores still steer every gate. Dustfall Refinery agrees
to read the one you carry, if you keep the Dune Marauders off its relay while
it does.

### 4. Cartographers of the storm · Ion Reach · ◆ 774
Scan ×3 → recover → courier: *Align beacon alpha → beta → gamma → Extract the storm log → Deliver the survey log*

The storm log of the Arc Spire holds the last guild chart: the route to
Eclipse runs through Obsidian, and the Iron Dominion holds it shut.

### 5. Break the iron blockade · Obsidian · ◆ 1170
Scan → hunt → elite → courier: *Identify the blockade command ship → Thin the blockade → Disable the Iron commander → Transmit the liberated route*

The blockade breaks. Bastion Dock opens its gates for the first time in a
generation, and the route to the outer worlds is free.

### 6. Under frozen skies · Cryosphere · ◆ 1170
Mine → defend → escort → courier: *Gather frozen relic ore → Protect the extraction relay → Escort the sample freighter → Deliver the cold-storage manifest*

Relic ore from the ice still carries guild markings. The manifest names the
last ship that carried the Charter: a guild cruiser bound for the Last Light
Citadel.

### 7. Heart of the forge · Nova Forge · ◆ 1800
Scan → hunt → boss → recover → courier: *Locate the flagship reactor → Destroy reactor pickets → Destroy the Ember flagship → Recover the reactor core → Deliver the reactor core*

Only a guild reactor core can light the Eclipse gate. The Ember Legion built
its flagship around one.

### 8. A light beyond Eclipse · Eclipse · ◆ 2880
Scan → defend → boss → recover → courier: *Reveal the Void signature → Keep the last-light relay active → Destroy the Void Dreadnought → Recover the frontier archive → Bring the archive home*

The frontier archive is the Charter. It is torn, but every world's seal is
still on it. **End of act I:** the Charter is home in Haven, and it is worth
nothing until the worlds sign it again.

## Act II. The Charter

Each world asks for something before it signs. A world signs only when it
trusts you (see Reputation in [README.md](README.md)), so act II is also a
journey of reputation.

### 9. Routes worth defending · Haven · ◆ 1200
Scan → escort → elite → courier: *Trace a stolen convoy → Protect the relief convoy → Break the pursuit → Report the safe route*

Haven signs first, if its relief convoy gets through. The first signature.

### 10. The living archive · Verdant · ◆ 1450
Recover → defend → courier: *Retrieve the seed archive → Keep the archive relay online → Deliver to cryogenic storage*

Verdant asks you to save its seed archive, the memory of a living world.

### 11. A profitable detour · Dustfall · ◆ 1700
Mine → trade → escort: *Extract refinery ore → Supply the Iron foundry → Protect the outbound carrier*

Dustfall signs when trade with Obsidian runs again. Old rivals have to sell
to each other.

### 12. Stormbreaker network · Ion Reach · ◆ 1850
Scan → elite → defend → courier: *Align the storm relay → Eliminate the Volt marshal → Hold the cold relay → Return the network key*

The Volt Collective jams every signal of the Arc Spire. The network key
brings the guild frequency back.

### 13. The shattered armistice · Obsidian · ◆ 2150
Hunt → elite → recover → courier: *Break the occupation fleet → Defeat the Dominion admiral → Recover the treaty → Deliver the treaty*

Obsidian's old armistice with its neighbours is the model for the Charter's
peace clause.

### 14. Pilgrims of the ice · Cryosphere · ◆ 2350
Scan → escort → defend → courier: *Find the lost pilgrims → Protect the pilgrim freighter → Defend the resupply relay → Return the pilgrim manifest*

The Glacier Relay signs when its pilgrims come home.

### 15. A second sunrise · Nova Forge · ◆ 2700
Mine → elite → boss → courier: *Collect reactor material → Defeat the Forge warden → Silence the Helios warship → Deliver the reactor blueprint*

With a second reactor the forge can power the guild's own gate network.

### 16. Beyond the last light · Eclipse · ◆ 3400
Scan → elite → defend → boss → recover → courier: *Chart the outer darkness → Break the Void honor guard → Stabilize the frontier beacon → Defeat the reborn dreadnought → Recover the expedition charter → Found the expedition guild*

The last signature is Eclipse's own. **End of act II:** the Expedition Guild
is founded in Haven, and the frontier expeditions open.

## Career lines (new)

Three chapters each, optional, open with rank. Each ends with a guild seat:
a title shown in the career tab, and a lasting perk.

### Trader: the Merchants' seat · from rank 1 (Courier)
1. **Cheap ore, dear cells.** Buy ore in Dustfall, sell it in Ion Reach, and come back with cells: one full trade loop, with a profit target.
2. **The market war.** Two worlds bid against each other for relic components; sell to one of them, and lose reputation with the other.
3. **The sealed contract.** A merchant offers a fortune for sealed cargo through Obsidian. Inspect it and refuse, or carry it as a smuggler past the Bastion Guard (an outlaw choice).

*Seat:* market prices 5% better in every world where you are Established or higher.

### Escort: the Convoy Masters' seat · from rank 2 (Pathfinder)
1. **One freighter, one gate.** Escort a freighter from Haven through the gate to Verdant.
2. **The long convoy.** Three freighters from Dustfall to Cryosphere, through four worlds; pay by cargo saved.
3. **The ambush at the Bastion.** A convoy is attacked at the Obsidian gate; hold the gate until the guards arrive.

*Seat:* escorted freighters have stronger shields; convoys pay 20% more.

### Hunter: the Hunters' seat · from rank 2 (Pathfinder)
1. **Wanted in Haven.** The Rim Corsairs' ace, with a bounty on a wanted list.
2. **The marauder's trail.** Track a Dune Marauder captain across three worlds.
3. **The Void marshal.** An elite of the Void Armada who hunts guild pilots.

*Seat:* bounties pay 25% more; the wanted lists of all eight worlds show on the map.

### Warden of the lanes: the Guards' seat · from rank 3 (Vanguard)
1. **Patrol duty.** Fly a full patrol route with the Anchorage Patrol and fight what you meet.
2. **Inspection.** Stop three ships at the Haven gate; one of them is a smuggler.
3. **Amnesty.** Bring a Wanted pilot in to pay their fine instead of destroying them.

*Seat:* guards of every world where you are Established or higher help you in a fight.

### Surveyor: the Cartographers' seat · from rank 4 (Ace)
1. **The silent beacon.** A beacon that is on no map, in Cryosphere.
2. **Charts for sale.** Map a sector and sell the chart to two worlds.
3. **Beyond the edge.** The first frontier expedition flown under the guild's name.

*Seat:* expeditions show their objectives on arrival; survey contracts pay more.
