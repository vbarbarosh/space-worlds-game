# World Explorer: findings met on the way, 2026-11-14

Repository: `vbarbarosh/space-worlds-game`, branch `main`, HEAD `28361aa` (2026-11-14). Not a sweep: four things met while a night's changes were landed and timed (recorded sounds, Haven's task, the look's first touches, the strategy part's removal). The full audit is `audit-2026-11-10.md`; its ids go on here.

## Scope

Only what the night's own checks ran into: `bin/perf` while two drawing changes were timed, `bin/layout-check` on the HUD, the station and the maps at every window size, and the news line under an old save's pay-back. Nothing else was read for faults.

### Checks and limitations

Run on the same 12-core Linux container as the full audit, software drawing, with other browsers at work on the box for most of the night (the load is written beside each number).

- `bin/perf --mode=frames`, `--mode=split` and `--mode=raster` on `calm-w0-z100`, `fight-w0-z100`, `arcade-w2-z100`, four runs a page, two pages in turn, 1-minute load 6 to 14.
- `bin/layout-check` on 15 screens at fifteen sizes: 644 of 645 rows pass (WE-319 is the one).
- `bin/play-check`: 19 of 19.

## Verdict

Nothing here stops play. Two are the timing tool's: its fight and arcade scenes are not the same from run to run though its help says so, and a pass's time in its split mode is not that pass's cost on a software canvas; together they can name the wrong pass as slow, and did once that night before whole frames were compared. Two are small faults of the screen.

## Findings

**Tools and checks**

### WE-317 bin/perf's fight and arcade scenes differ from run to run, and its help says they are the same (medium)

- Where: `bin/perf:66`
- Kind: wrong; seen by: operator
- The line: `on a virtual clock of 60 frames a second and a seeded Math.random, so a scene is the same on every run.`
- Evidence: measured: one page, `fight-w0-z100`, four runs in `--mode=frames`: the frame's middle time 3.0, 23.2, 3.5 and 9.4 ms; two `--mode=shots` runs of one page differed in 99.9% of the canvas's pixels in `fight-w0-z100` and by the score in `arcade-w2-z100` (2950 and 2925). The calm scenes repeat (two runs differ by a pixel).
- What goes wrong: One before and one after of a fight scene proves nothing, and reads as proof.
- Mend (medium): find what a fight draws on that is not the seeded dice or the virtual clock; until then the help says which scenes repeat

### WE-318 A pass's time in bin/perf's split mode holds the canvas's own drawing of earlier passes (low)

- Where: `bin/perf:72`
- Kind: wrong; seen by: operator
- The line: `split    the same, with the time of each render pass`
- Evidence: measured: `fight-w0-z100` in `--mode=split`, the minimap's 64 icons a frame read 3.5 to 12 ms a frame on one page and 12 to 21 ms with a change that draws 28 small pictures far earlier in the frame; drawing the icons unscaled changed nothing (four pages, four rounds). In `--mode=raster` the two pages' whole frames were 34 and 28 ms: no slower.
- What goes wrong: The pass that draws many pictures late in a frame is taken for the slow one, and a change elsewhere for its cause.
- Mend (small): say it in the help; point at `--mode=raster` for a comparison and `--mode=layers` for a pass's cost

**Screens**

### WE-319 In a Scroll lane's flagship wave an event's news line and the flagship's bar overlap by 6 px, now and then (low)

- Where: `src/css/ui-kit.css:620`, `bin/layout-check:17`
- Kind: wrong; seen by: user
- The line: `.ui .hud-toasts > * { max-width: 100%; pointer-events: auto; }`
- Evidence: measured: `bin/layout-check hud-arcade-scroll-edge` (Eclipse, a Scroll lane, the flagship's wave) failed two runs of four with `overlap .hud-toasts > * × .hud-toasts > * (77×6px)`, at 1280x720 in one and at 943x980 in the other; the screenshot shows an ace's line ("Lieutenant") over the flagship's bar. Seen on the code before and after the strategy part's removal.
- What goes wrong: The news line's lower edge covers the top of the flagship's bar for as long as the line stays.
- Mend (small): give the two their own rows in the toast area

### WE-320 A news line stays 3.5 s at most, whatever its caller asks: the pay-back of an old save asks for 8 (low)

- Where: `src/js/hud.js:72`, `src/js/campaign.js:146`
- Kind: wrong; seen by: user
- The line: `news.life = clamp(Math.min(duration + 0.5, 1.2 + (news.title.length + news.sub.length)*0.03), 1.6, 3.5);`
- Evidence: read, and seen once: an old save with drones and outposts continued: "Mining drones and outposts are gone", with what was paid back, waits behind the arrival's banner and then shows for 3.5 s; its text is cut with an ellipsis and whole only in the tooltip.
- What goes wrong: The one message that says where a player's drones and outposts went is gone before it is read.
- Mend (small): let a caller hold a line longer, or say this one in the station's news too
