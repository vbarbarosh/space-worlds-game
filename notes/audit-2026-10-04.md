## 1. Keys

### WE-01 One R press docked and undocked again (high, fixed)
`src/js/controls.js:172` docks on R (`interact` → `dock_station`), then the same event reaches `src/js/station.js:404`, where R means Undock. The station flashed open and shut ("the map just blinks"). Fixed: `dock_station` stamps `station_docked_at`, and `on_station_key` ignores a key pressed before it. Tested: R undocks, R docks, the station stays open.

### WE-05 Two keys dock: the card shows T, everything else shows R (high)
In docking range the guide's action is `'R / DOCK NOW'` (`src/js/guidance.js:242`). The mission card takes the label but gives it the T chip (`src/js/hud.js:250`), so the card says **[T] Dock now**. Meanwhile:
- the toolbar says **Dock [R]** (`src/html/hud.html`, `src/js/navigation.js:421`);
- the station's plate says "Station · R dock" (`src/js/surfaces.js:719`);
- the guide's text says "press R to dock" (`src/js/guide.js:202, 225, 298`).

Both keys work, so the screen teaches two keys for one act. Evidence: the user's screenshot 20261004_134503, and files/20261004_135204-agent-two-dock-keys.png.

Recommendation, one rule:
- **R** acts on the place you are at (dock, jump, the arcade's depot). Its button stays in the toolbar.
- **T** goes to the next step (fly to the marker, open the map when nothing is chosen).
- In range, the card's button becomes **[R] Dock now**, so T never docks.

The other way round (T everywhere, the R button and plates dropped) is also consistent, but then the gate and station plates need a new hint, and R stays bound with nothing showing it.

### WE-06 Follow buttons show a T that does not follow that contract (medium)
Each taken contract's Follow button carries a T chip (`src/js/station.js:172`). T in the station runs the NEXT strip's action (`src/js/station.js:401`), not that contract's Follow. With three contracts, three buttons claim the same key. Drop the chip from Follow.

### WE-12 Local jump gates say "R jump"; you fly through them (low)
`src/js/render.js:78` (added in the last round) labels jump gates A–H "R jump". They teleport when you fly into the ring; R does nothing there (help text, `src/js/guidance.js:471`). The plate should say "fly through".

### WE-13 The inventory says "I / Tab close"; I opens mission details (low)
`src/html/inventory.html` ends with "Q repair · E EMP · F stasis · I / Tab close". Since the HUD redesign I toggles the mission card's Details (`src/js/hud.js:35`), and only in flight. Only Tab closes the inventory.

## 2. Arcade against campaign

The user chose Arcade and found "missions again". The arcade plays as it did before the redesign (waves, depot, flagship). But it shows and opens the campaign's tools:

### WE-02 J opens the campaign's map & guide in the arcade (high)
`toggle_navigation` (`src/js/guidance.js:406`) has no arcade check; J (`src/js/controls.js:175`) and the minimap's "Open J" open it. Inside the arcade:
- "Choose a destination · Select a mission or click a map destination";
- every world "upgrades required";
- cargo 0/40, trade intel, the mission plan.

Evidence: files/20261004_135204-agent-arcade-map.png.

### WE-03 The arcade's card is the campaign's detail lines (high)
The arcade shows the mission card's Details block (`src/html/hud.html` `#mission_details`, `src/js/hud.js` `sync_hud_kit`):
- coordinates;
- follow state and the gate cooldown;
- "Open J MAP & GUIDE. Select a mission…";
- the world rule;
- the flight readout;
- World rules and Brake.

The arcade needs its own card: world, wave n of 3 with its bar, raiders left, the flagship, and the depot [R] when raiders are clear.

### WE-04 Campaign labels in the arcade's world (medium)
In the arcade:
- world gates say "R jump · upgrades required" (`src/js/navigation.js:463`), but worlds change by clearing waves;
- mining fields show their ore (`src/js/render.js:38`), but there is no mining;
- the station says "R dock" (`src/js/surfaces.js:719`), but R opens the depot.

## 3. Guide

### WE-07 Docked, the guide says to fly to the station and dock (medium)
With a world chosen as the route and nothing else, `src/js/guide.js:295-299` gives "You are in Haven. Follow the gold marker to Haven Anchorage and press R to dock" and "Fly to station". It does this whatever `effective_state` is, so the station's NEXT strip says it while you are docked, with a **[T] Fly to station** button. Evidence: the user's screenshot 20261004_134724.

## 4. Words and case

### WE-08 "One slot free", then "Slot free", "Slot free" (low)
`src/js/station.js:185` writes the first empty slot differently from the rest. One wording for every empty slot ("Free slot"), and the count is already in the heading ("Taken 0 of 3").

### WE-09 Five names for the map (medium)
- toolbar "Map & guide [J]";
- guide text "Open J MAP & GUIDE" (`src/js/guide.js:118`);
- status line "R DOCK · J NAVIGATION" (`src/js/navigation.js:416`);
- minimap "Open J";
- card button "[T] Open map", while J also opens it.

Settle on "Map & guide (J)".

### WE-10 Several names for one thing (medium)
- **contract / mission / operation:**
  - the tab is CONTRACTS;
  - the guide says "Select a mission" (`src/js/guide.js:118`);
  - buttons say "GUIDE THIS MISSION", "OPEN ACTIVE MISSION PLAN" (`src/js/guidance.js:369`);
  - a toast says "OPERATION COMPLETE".
- **ship / shuttle:** "SHUTTLE NOT PREPARED" (`src/js/navigation.js:190`), "Prepare your shuttle" (`src/js/guide.js:171`), "Your shuttle is ready" (`src/js/goals.js:31`). It is your ship everywhere else.
- **Modules / Outfitter:** the station tab is MODULES, but the guide sends you to "OUTFITTER", which does not exist: `src/js/guide.js:167` "OPEN OUTFITTER", `src/js/navigation.js:190` "DOCK AT THE OUTFITTER", `src/js/guidance.js:479`.
- **defense / defence:** "DEFENSE" in ratings and help, "defence" in the map's summary (`src/js/navigation.js:88`, last round) and "DEFENCE PLATFORM" (`src/js/structures.js:429`).

### WE-11 Capitals and sentence case mixed, often in one line (medium)
The kit screens write sentence case. The guide's actions ("FLY TO OBJECTIVE", "OPEN CARGO MARKET"), 64 toasts, status lines and help cards are written in capitals at the source. Some are lowered on the way (toasts through `ui_sentence`, the card's button), others are not. So one line mixes both:
- "Open J MAP & GUIDE. Select a mission…";
- "Press UNDOCK, then FLY TO NEXT MARKER";
- the map's "OPEN MAP" button beside "Return to flight".

Write player-facing strings in sentence case at the source, and keep capitals to the kit's eyebrows, which CSS sets.

### WE-14 The world count written three ways (low)
The readout says "01 / 8", the kit and the map say "1/8" and "1/8 worlds visited", the station says "story 0/16".

## 5. Look

### WE-15 Seven screens and the map's tab bodies still on the old look (medium)
On the kit: HUD, station, map & guide frame and its mission plan, pause, main menu. Still old (capitals, rounded old buttons, centred text):
- save / load (`src/html/saves.html`);
- build (`build.html`);
- inventory (`inventory.html`);
- sound settings (`audio.html`);
- results / ship lost (`result.html`);
- the arcade depot (`arcade.html`);
- in the map: the briefing card, the world and local map controls, trade intel, flight guide and world rules cards.

All fit every window size, but they read as another game.

## What is good
- The layout holds at every size: no block over another, nothing off the window (bin/layout-check, 132 of 132).
- Every action has a button that shows its key (the quick bar, toolbar, station footer, pause).
- The station and the map number their tabs, and the number keys open them.
- Toasts are already lowered to sentence case on the way to the screen, so WE-11 is half done for them.

## Recommended order of work
1. Arcade apart from the campaign: WE-02, WE-03, WE-04. One arcade card; J and the map closed in the arcade; campaign plates hidden.
2. One key per act: WE-05, WE-06, WE-12, WE-13. R acts at a place, T goes to the next step; chips only where the key does that.
3. Guide state: WE-07.
4. One glossary and sentence case at the source: WE-08, WE-09, WE-10, WE-11, WE-14. Contract, ship, Modules, Map & guide, defense.
5. The old screens onto the kit: WE-15.
