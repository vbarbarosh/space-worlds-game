# Lure the Leviathan

World: Verdant · Reward: 900

A Leviathan has come out of the deep lanes and hunts anything that moves in Verdant. No gun on the frontier can scratch its hull. The Canopy Exchange's plan is older than the guild: lead it into a black hole, where its own mass will finish it.

Fly to the marked black hole and it will come for you. Keep ahead of it, skirt the pull, ride the living currents if they help, and let the core do the rest. Mind the core yourself.
