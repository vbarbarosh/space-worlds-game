# A pilot earns their wings

World: Haven · Reward: 306

Haven Anchorage hands out licences only to pilots who can bring ore it can trust. Calibrate the mining scanner at the beacon and drill a trial load; the dock master reads the analysis when you dock.

The dock master tells you about the old guild that once flew these lanes, and about the gate to Verdant that nobody uses any more.
