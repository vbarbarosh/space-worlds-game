# A pilot earns their wings

World: Haven · Reward: 306

Haven Anchorage hands out licences only to pilots who can bring ore it can trust. Calibrate the mining scanner at the beacon, drill a trial load, and bring the analysis home.

The dock master tells you about the old guild that once flew these lanes, and about the gate to Verdant that nobody uses any more.
